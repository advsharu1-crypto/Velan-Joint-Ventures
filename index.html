Good partnerships begin with the right conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions worth asking early.

Every opportunity is unique. These answers provide general information, not legal, tax or investment advice.

Ask a Specific Question

A joint venture is a business arrangement where two or more parties collaborate on a defined activity or business objective while agreeing how responsibilities, resources, control and returns will be handled.

Business owners, companies, project developers and other organisations exploring a strategic collaboration can submit an enquiry for an initial discussion.

Partner identification may be considered based on the opportunity, target profile, available network and agreed scope. No partner match or outcome is guaranteed.

Commercial discussion points can be organised as part of a structured process. Formal legal advice, drafting and review should be undertaken by appropriately qualified legal professionals.

Start with your company or organisation, sector, broad objective, preferred collaboration type and the stage of your opportunity. Avoid sending confidential documents through an unsecured form.

No business outcome, partner match, funding, return or transaction can be guaranteed. Each opportunity depends on its circumstances, due diligence and decisions by the parties involved.
